PokéSurvivor Pokédex: all 966 playable Pokémon

Every one of these Pokémon can be your partner in PokéSurvivor, the free Pokémon survival game for your browser and phone. There are 966 from all 9 regions; you get them as a starter or from the gacha (with tickets you earn by playing), and they also show up as wild enemies, bosses and legendaries in rifts.

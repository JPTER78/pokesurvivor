PokéSurvivor guide

PokéSurvivor is a free Pokémon survival game in the style of Survivor.io and Vampire Survivors that you play in your browser: pick a partner, roam an endless map and your moves fire on their own while hordes of wild Pokémon try to surround you. How long can you last?

How to play

  • On PC: move with WASD or the arrow keys. Switch moves with 1–4, Q/E or by clicking their icon. Pause with Esc.
  • On phone or tablet (landscape): drag your finger anywhere to move, tap a move to switch to it and the top-right button to pause.
  • Your Pokémon attacks on its own with the active move. Your job is to dodge and position yourself well.
  • Collect experience to level up and pick one of three cards: new moves (from the ones your Pokémon knows), upgrades to the ones you have, or boosts for your Pokémon.
  • In a group: quick messages with 5–8 (Help!, Over here!, Rift!, Thanks!) or the speech bubble on mobile.

What a run is like

  • 3 acts of 10 minutes, each in a random biome: Forest, Cave, Volcano, Lavender Tower (fog and ghosts) or Desert (quicksand and sandstorms). Wild Pokémon are usually of the biome’s types.
  • At the end of each act a zone boss arrives: beat it to move on. The act 3 one is the final boss, a legendary with phases. Beat it and a portal opens: step through and you win.
  • Along the way: roaming bosses (sometimes Alpha, huge with red eyes, or Totem, calling for help), legendary rifts, chests, berry trees (each biome with its own berry and power), risk altars and events like mass outbreaks (more shinies) or a roaming Pokémon.
  • Ranks: winning in Normal unlocks Bronze, then Silver, Gold and Master. Each is harder and gives more rewards, and under “Play” you choose which one to play (in a group, the host picks, up to the highest in the group). In Master, if you don’t step through the portal, endless mode begins, which is what the leaderboard counts.

Your Pokémon, between runs

  • Each Pokémon has its own level (Lv., up to 100), which goes up with the experience from each run. As it levels up it learns moves, the same as in the main games, and only those (plus the ones you teach it with TMs) show up in your cards.
  • It has its real stats (physical moves use Attack and special ones Sp. Atk), a nature and one of its own abilities.
  • After a few runs the Kecleon Shop opens: candies to level up, TMs, Mints (change the nature) and Ability Capsules. Everything goes to your Bag.
  • Account Upgrades (Magnet, Wisdom, Fortune, Critical…) apply to all your Pokémon.

Features

  • 966 Pokémon from all 9 regions (see the Pokédex), each with its own level, stats, nature and ability.
  • 575 moves from the main games, and each Pokémon learns its own. They evolve: at max level with the right card, Ember becomes Fire Blast, Bubble becomes Surf…
  • Enemies with personality: some Pokémon heal, shield their allies, summon help, explode or fire telegraphed beams.
  • Bosses and rifts with 18 legendary arenas, one per type.
  • Weather and type chart: in the rain Water gets stronger and Fire weaker; plus sun, sand, snow and fog.
  • Wild shinies (1 in 4096) and in the gacha.
  • A slot-machine gacha for each region, with tickets you earn by playing.
  • Held items such as Leftovers, Choice Band and Life Orb.
  • Online co-op for up to 4 with your friends list.
  • Daily and weekly missions, 131 achievements with medals and titles, a public profile and leaderboards.
  • In English and Spanish, on PC and mobile, and installable as an app.

Tips to survive longer

  1. Don't stand still: move in wide circles so the hordes follow you in a line.
  2. Use the type chart: switch to the move that's super effective against what's in front of you.
  3. Markers on the ground (circles and lines filling up) are attacks about to land: step out of them.
  4. Take out healers and shield-casters first.
  5. Check your Pokémon’s card: if it’s better with physical or special moves, pick cards of that class.
  6. Level up your favorite with candies: it learns new moves sooner and lasts longer.
  7. Save some health for the zone boss: if you take too long to beat it, it gets enraged.

Frequently asked questions

Is PokéSurvivor free?

Yes, completely. It's a non-profit fan game with no ads or purchases. If you like, you can support it with a voluntary donation, which only pays for the servers and gives no advantages.

Do I need to download anything?

No. You play in your browser on PC, tablet or phone. If you like, you can install it as an app with “Add to Home Screen”.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes, in landscape. Put your finger anywhere on the screen and drag to move (the joystick appears where you touch), tap a move to switch to it and the top-right button to pause.

Is it in English?

Yes: the game shows up in English or Spanish depending on your browser language, and you can change it in Settings.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes: online co-op supports up to 4 players. Add your friends by nickname, invite them to your room and start the game. Experience is shared, you can revive a fallen teammate by standing next to them and there are quick messages (Help!, Over here!…). You play the highest rank anyone in the group has.

How do you win a run?

A run has 3 acts of 10 minutes, each in a random biome. At the end of each act a zone boss arrives; the third one is a legendary with phases. Beat it and a portal opens: step through and you win. Each victory unlocks the next rank (Bronze, Silver, Gold and Master), harder and with bigger rewards.

What is endless mode?

In the Master rank, if you beat the final boss and don't step through the portal, endless waves begin, stronger and stronger. The leaderboard counts how long you last there.

How does my Pokémon level up?

Each Pokémon has its own level (up to 100), which goes up with the experience it earns in each run or with candies from the Kecleon Shop. As it levels up it learns the same moves as in the main games, and TMs can teach it others. It also has its own nature and ability.

How many Pokémon are there?

966 Pokémon from all 9 regions (Kanto to Paldea), all playable as your partner, with their shiny form when one exists.

How do I get more Pokémon?

From the gacha, which uses tickets you earn by playing: regular Pokémon drop tickets now and then, and bosses and rift legendaries sometimes drop ×10 tickets. They can't be bought with real money.

What are rifts?

Mysterious portals that open now and then during the acts. If you go in, you fight a legendary Pokémon in an arena of its type (there are 18). Win and you get a reward and a bond to fire several moves at once; fall and you're sent back to the map.

Is my progress saved?

With an account (name and password, or Google) your game is saved in the cloud and you can continue on any device. Guests can play right away, but nothing is saved.

Is this an official Pokémon game?

No. It's a free, non-profit fan project, unrelated to Nintendo, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.

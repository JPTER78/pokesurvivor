PokéSurvivor moves and their evolutions

There are 575 moves from the main games, across all 18 types. Each Pokémon learns its own as it levels up (the same as in the main games) and can learn others with TMs. In a run it attacks on its own with the active one, and when you level up you pick cards to learn more of the ones it knows or upgrade them. Physical moves use its Attack and special ones its Sp. Atk; status moves deal no damage but boost your Pokémon or weaken enemies. 486 of them can evolve: max them out, get the card they ask for (Power, Vigor, Reflexes…) and a golden evolution card will appear.

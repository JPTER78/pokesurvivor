PokéSurvivor moves and their evolutions

There are 103 moves across all 18 types. Your Pokémon attacks on its own with the active one, and when you level up you pick cards to learn new moves or upgrade them up to level 5. 97 of them can evolve: max them out, get the upgrade card they ask for (Power, Vigor, Reflexes…) and a golden evolution card will appear.

Every move is physical or special: it hits according to your Pokémon's Attack or Special Attack, and the target's Defense or Special Defense softens it. With TMs from the Kecleon Shop (8 new ones every day) you can permanently teach a move outside its types, if your Pokémon learns it in the games.

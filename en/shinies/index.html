Shinies in PokéSurvivor: how to get them

Shiny Pokémon are rare, differently colored versions. In PokéSurvivor almost all 966 Pokémon have a shiny form, with real sprites.

How to get them

  • In the wild: every Pokémon that spawns has a 1 in 4096 chance of being shiny (just like the classic games). You'll see it sparkle and get an alert: defeat it and it's yours.
  • From the gacha: every pull has a 1 in 100 chance of being shiny.
  • A duplicate shiny gives triple PokéCoins.
  • Once you own your partner's shiny form, you can play with it (pick it in your collection).

Support PokéSurvivor

PokéSurvivor is and will stay free: no ads, no purchases and no pay-to-win. If you enjoy it and want to help keep it online, you can make a voluntary donation. All the money goes to the servers and the domain; anything left over is saved for the following months.

Donating gives no advantage in the game: everyone plays on equal terms.

What it costs to run

Servers (saves, accounts, leaderboards and multiplayer)Depends on how many people play

The servers store saves and accounts, the leaderboards and group games: the more people play, the more they cost.
